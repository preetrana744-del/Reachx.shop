OUR OWN WEBSITE · FIRST-PARTY IMPLEMENTATION

A working reference you can inspect

This walkthrough describes ReachX.shop itself. It is an internal website example, not a client case study, testimonial or traffic-growth claim.

The task

Connect a visual agency homepage to detailed service information and a clear project conversation. The public site needs readable content as well as its animated presentation.

What is implemented

  • Native links from the homepage service cards to detailed service pages.
  • Distinct page titles, descriptions and canonical URLs, supported by an XML sitemap.
  • Service-specific project briefs, related guides and an explanation of scope boundaries.
  • A project-enquiry form with confirmation and email/WhatsApp alternatives.

Inspect the result

Homepage and service cards · SEO service and brief · Business guides · Enquiry journey · Sitemap

What this demonstrates

The links, content and enquiry journey are observable implementation work. They do not prove improved rankings, AI citations, sales or client outcomes. Those results require a dated baseline and sufficient measurement.

The separate fictional consultant example is a teaching aid and remains labelled as fictional.
