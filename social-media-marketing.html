Social media marketing with a clear purpose

A social presence is easier to plan when you know whom you want to reach and what you want people to remember. Reach X offers social media marketing across Instagram and Facebook, with content strategy and campaign creative. The aim is a consistent presence built around your audience, your business and the message you want to share, rather than a collection of unrelated posts.

Discuss your project ↗

Give your content a job to do

Some posts introduce your business. Others explain an offer, answer a familiar question or give someone a reason to get in touch. A content strategy brings those purposes together, so your feed can tell a coherent story. Start by listing the topics customers ask about, the services or products you want to explain and the moments in your business that are worth sharing.

Make campaigns recognisable and relevant

Campaign creative should connect the message with the audience and the next step. A product introduction, a service explanation and a seasonal offer need different information, even when they share your brand's visual style. Bring any existing photos, brand guidance and examples of content you like. Describe the audience you want to speak to and whether the campaign should support awareness, enquiries or another specific business objective.

BEFORE WE BEGIN

Your questions, answered

Do I need to use both Instagram and Facebook?

The appropriate focus depends on your audience and goals. Share the accounts you already use and where customers currently interact with your business. That gives us a practical starting point for discussing the scope.

What if I do not have a content plan yet?

Start with your offers, common customer questions and the people you want to reach. You do not need a finished plan to enquire; content strategy is part of the social media service.

Does a social media enquiry include paid advertising?

Explain whether you are asking about ongoing content, campaign creative or paid campaigns. These can involve different work and costs, so the specific scope and any advertising budget should be discussed before a project begins.
