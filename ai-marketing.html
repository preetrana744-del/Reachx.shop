BEFORE WE BEGIN

Your questions, answered

Will AI create and publish everything by itself?

The approach is human-led. AI assistance supports the work, while people review the direction and content. Decisions about your offer, business facts and final messaging should remain connected to someone who understands the business.

Where is AI assistance most useful for a small team?

A useful starting point is a recurring task that takes time and has a clear review process, such as organising content ideas or preparing initial drafts. Choose a manageable task before expanding the workflow to more of your marketing activity.

Can AI-assisted marketing guarantee better search rankings?

No. AI assistance is a way to support research, content and workflows; it does not control search rankings. Marketing decisions should prioritise useful information for your audience, accurate business details and a clear customer experience.
