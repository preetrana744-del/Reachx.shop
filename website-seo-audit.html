A useful review, around your goal

This starting review suits owners of English-language service-business websites, including consultants and small teams. Share your main service, the customers you want to reach and one question you want the review to address.

Request a short personalised video explaining three priority observations and a suggested next step. Findings are based on the public pages reviewed; a full-site audit and implementation require an agreed scope.

What the initial review covers

We look at a small set of relevant pages: your main service page and the path to an enquiry. We consider the offer, headings, mobile reading experience and contact journey, alongside titles, crawlable links and public indexing signals.

Private analytics, Search Console access and technical changes are outside this initial public-page review. Public page checks alone cannot confirm how Google has indexed a site.

A PRACTICAL REVIEW

Turn observations into priorities

Does the offer make sense?

Can a visitor understand whom you help, what you provide and why the service fits their problem?

Can the visitor take the next step?

Do contact links, page flow and mobile presentation make the enquiry journey clear?

Do the page signals match its topic?

Do the title, main heading, service copy and internal links describe the same offer? Are there visible discovery issues to investigate?
