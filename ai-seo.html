Traditional SEO, AEO and GEO: where they overlap

SEO supports discovery in search. AEO focuses on answering customer questions clearly, while GEO describes visibility in generative search experiences. The work shares the same foundation: accessible pages, useful evidence, descriptive internal links and accurate business information. Google states that its AI features require no special AI markup or new machine-readable file.

How we assess progress

A manually observed AI mention is a sample for that query and date, not proof of consistent placement or attributable revenue.

AI SEO QUESTIONS

Clear answers before you begin

What is AI SEO?

AI SEO is an extension of modern SEO focused on making useful web content easy for AI-driven search and answer systems to understand. It combines technical SEO, clear entities, strong information architecture and content that directly answers relevant user needs.

Is AEO or GEO completely different from traditional SEO?

No. Strong foundations overlap heavily. Crawlability, useful content, internal linking, structured data and clear page purpose still matter. AI-search work adds attention to how information is organised, how entities and relationships are expressed, and how complex questions are answered.

Can Reach X guarantee that my business appears in AI Overviews or AI answers?

No. Search and AI systems decide which sources to use for each query. Reach X can improve the technical and content foundations that make a website easier to discover and understand, but no specific placement or citation can be guaranteed.

Do I need llms.txt or special AI schema to appear in Google AI features?

Google says no new AI text file or special schema is required for its AI search features. Pages still need to meet normal search requirements, be indexed and be eligible for a snippet. A discovery file alone does not guarantee visibility.

Can AI SEO guarantee ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity citations?

No. Each platform chooses sources independently and results can vary by query, time and user context. ReachX can improve accessible, well-supported website content and review available evidence, but cannot promise citations or placement.
