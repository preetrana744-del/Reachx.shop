Give each landing page a clear job

A campaign page should explain the offer that brought the visitor there. It needs to answer basic questions about the service, who it suits and what happens after an enquiry. A focused page can then guide visitors toward one relevant action, such as requesting information or discussing their requirements, without making them search through unrelated content first.

Design the journey around enquiry quality

The goal is to attract conversations your business can actually help with. That means matching the audience, campaign promise and information requested. Too little context can produce enquiries that are difficult to follow up; too many questions can discourage someone who is just exploring. We consider the steps together and keep the information request proportionate to the decision being made.

BEFORE WE BEGIN

Your questions, answered

What counts as a useful lead?

A useful lead is someone whose needs fit your offer and who has shown a meaningful interest in discussing it. Define that fit around your services, customer requirements and ability to help, rather than judging success only by the number of submissions.

Can lead generation improve an existing website?

Yes. An existing website may already attract interested visitors but leave the next step unclear. Reviewing the offer, enquiry page and route to contact can reveal where people need more explanation or a simpler choice.

What should happen after someone enquires?

Your business should have a clear follow-up process. Let people know what to expect, respond to their actual question and explain the next step. A well-designed enquiry journey is most useful when the conversation continues promptly after submission.
