Choose the right kind of website

A business website gives people a place to learn about your services, explore your story and contact you. A landing page focuses on one offer or campaign, with a clear path to an enquiry. An e-commerce site helps people browse and consider products. Choosing the format before discussing visual details makes the brief easier to follow and helps identify which pages and features matter most.

Bring the information customers need

A useful project brief includes your services or products, the people you serve, your contact details and any existing brand materials. Think about the information a customer needs before getting in touch: what is included, who the offer suits and what happens next. Share examples of websites you find easy to use, alongside anything in your current site that confuses visitors or creates extra work for you.

BEFORE WE BEGIN

Your questions, answered

Do I need a full website or one landing page?

A landing page can suit one specific offer. A full website gives more room for several services, products or audiences. Describe your immediate goal and the information customers need so we can discuss the appropriate scope.

What should I prepare before contacting Reach X?

Share a short description of your business, your existing website if you have one, the pages or features you need and any brand materials. You can also mention a preferred launch date and budget.

Can we discuss improvements to an existing website?

Yes. Send the current link and explain what you want to improve. Existing content and design can provide a starting point for discussing a refreshed website and the work involved.
