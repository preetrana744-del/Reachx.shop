Start with the message your customers need

Good creative work begins with a clear understanding of the business. What do you offer, who is it for and what should someone remember? Answering those questions helps determine the visual direction and the words that accompany it. The aim is a presentation that fits your business and audience, with enough consistency to connect different pieces of content over time.

Make each creative useful in its setting

A social post and a campaign graphic have different jobs. One might introduce an idea; another might explain an offer or invite an enquiry. The creative needs a clear message, readable information and a sensible next step for that setting. Keeping these pieces aligned with your visual identity helps your presence feel professional while giving each asset a practical purpose.

BEFORE WE BEGIN

Your questions, answered

Can you work with our existing brand identity?

Share the identity, current assets and any usage guidance you already have. These provide a starting point for discussing what should stay consistent and what needs improvement. The scope can focus on new content that works with the direction you already use.

What should we provide for a creative project?

Useful inputs include your business description, audience, current visual assets, accurate service information and the purpose of the content. Share examples you like and explain why you like them, so the discussion goes beyond colours or visual style alone.

Do all our posts need to look identical?

No. Consistency means using a recognisable direction and a clear voice, while allowing different messages and formats. Content should remain easy to identify as yours without forcing every idea into the same arrangement.
